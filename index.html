About KNSDC

Building Community,
Celebrating Culture

Kalikapur Nabin Sangha Direct Contrac (KNSDC) is one of West Bengal's most vibrant community organisations, founded in 2020 with a vision to unite youth through culture, sports and social welfare.

Over four decades we've grown from a small neighbourhood club to a cornerstone of the Kalikapur community — hosting grand Laxmi Puja celebrations, dance competitions, sports meets and youth development programmes, blood donation camp, and greenery programs that have touched thousands of lives.
