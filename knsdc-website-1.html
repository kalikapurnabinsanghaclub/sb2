🏛️ WHO WE ARE

About KNSDC

Building Community,
Celebrating Culture

Kalikapur Nabin Sangha DC (KNSDC) is one of South Kolkata's most vibrant community organisations, founded in 1985 with a vision to unite youth through culture, sports and social welfare.

Over four decades we've grown from a small neighbourhood club to a cornerstone of the Kalikapur community — hosting grand Durga Puja celebrations, dance competitions, sports meets and youth development programmes that have touched thousands of lives.
